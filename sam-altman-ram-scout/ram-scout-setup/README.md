# The Sam Altman RAM Scout

_Find surplus PCs, compare their RAM and work out what you can bid._

A RAM auction workflow adapted to your buying area, budget and connected tools. Find lots, compare exact sold modules, calculate ceilings, prepare resale and reconcile actual results. You handle purchases and hardware.

## Skills in this pack

- **Set up your RAM scout** (`ram-scout-setup/SKILL.md`): Connect your tools and create a working first search.
- **Find computer lots near you** (`ram-scout-find-lots/SKILL.md`): Refresh relevant surplus lots with honest source coverage.
- **Evaluate the RAM and your bid** (`ram-scout-evaluate-lots/SKILL.md`): Match sold modules and calculate a valid maximum bid.
- **Prepare your purchase and resale** (`ram-scout-prepare-resale/SKILL.md`): Guide the physical handoff and prepare accurate listing drafts.
- **Work out your actual profit** (`ram-scout-reconcile-sales/SKILL.md`): Reconcile sales, costs, refunds and remaining inventory.
